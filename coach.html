ARIJIT BASU

Founder & Head Personal Trainer • CBA 9 Fitness

Arijit Basu is the Founder and Head Personal Trainer at CBA 9 Fitness, and an alumnus of the University of Calcutta based in Kolkata. Dedicated to stripping away fitness fads, Arijit delivers structured, science-backed personal training, customized workout and nutrition systems, and specialized rehabilitation protocols for ACL injuries, slip discs, and spondylitis. Having guided hundreds of athletes and fitness enthusiasts toward life-changing transformations, he provides relentless support, posture education, and custom periodized programming.
